# QuoteForge living spec

NASAKTION Quote Generator is a browser-based oil and gas commercial quotation workspace. It lets a user build sales, rental, and service quotes with configurable UoM, multiple currencies, optional tax, payment terms, delivery lead time, editable customer placeholders, and notes. Rental and service lines use daily or lump-sum charge bases; sales lines use their own per-unit or line-total pricing option. Every saved quote is persisted in MongoDB and appears in release history.

The app is branded **NASAKTION Quote Generator**, generated by Zekha Anendetha. A singleton Workspace company profile stores the editable legal company name, address, email, phone, and logo. New quotations inherit that profile as a snapshot; the Quote tab contains only quote-specific identity and commercial fields.

## Data model
- `Quote`: string id, editable quote number, selectable release date, execution status (`released`, `cancelled`, `executed`, `partial_executed`, plus legacy draft/issued), supplier snapshot and persisted company logo, preparer name/title, customer details/reference/delivery point, quotation subject, currency, manually saved USD conversion rate, tax settings, terms, internal notes, client-facing release note, quote-level commission, line items, subtotal, cost, tax, grand total, gross profit, and margin percentage. Commission is divided equally across all line items and included in their client-facing prices.
- `QuoteLineItem`: description plus printable detailed specification, category (`sales`, `rental`, `service`), charge type (`daily`, `lump_sum`) for rental/service lines, sales pricing (`unit`, `line_total`) for sales lines, UoM, quantity, duration days, price method (`sell_rate`, `margin`), direct sell rate or target gross margin percentage, cost rate, cost added value percentage, calculated cost add-on, commission allocation, line total and line cost. Margin pricing derives the sell value from fully loaded cost using `loaded cost / (1 - margin %)`. Cost added value increases internal cost basis for government tax, landing cost, and similar charges.

## Key flows
1. Dashboard (`/`) shows persistent quote history and clickable USD-normalized revenue, cost, profit, and weighted margin metrics. Non-USD quotes require a saved manual `1 currency = X USD` rate; missing rates are excluded and flagged. Metric dialogs list every quotation. History includes preparer, original/USD values, editable execution status, open, and permanent delete actions.
2. New quote (`/quotes/new`) inherits the persistent Workspace company profile and captures only quote-specific parties, line items, release identity, settings, and notes. Totals and P&L update live.
3. Save creates a persistent quote and opens its saved URL (`/quotes/:id`). Saved quotes can be edited and saved again.
4. Print / PDF uses the browser print dialog and produces a true A4 customer release pack: commercial quotation, Quotation Terms and Conditions, and Customer Acceptance. The editor is completely hidden from print; release is enabled only after saving and completing subject/date/preparer details. Long tables flow automatically onto extra A4 pages with repeating table headings. A fixed print-only document header repeats the logo, commercial title, subject, confidentiality, reference, date, and validity on every physical overflow page. Author-defined empty top/side page margin boxes suppress browser date/title/URL metadata, while the bottom-center box renders `CONFIDENTIAL · Page X of Y`. A separate top-bar Download PDF action creates the same customer-only release directly with jsPDF/AutoTable, repeated headers on overflow pages, terms and acceptance pages, and explicit page numbering. Its filename is `<quotation-number>-<sanitized-subject>.pdf`. The first page includes an editable subject, generated introduction, customer reference and delivery point, line-item table, TOTAL, notes, and a Best Regards signature box. Terms derive price basis from currency, payment terms from the editable field, delivery timing from the parties section, and scope from subject; fixed order-confirmation/change-order clauses are included. Acceptance includes the legal acknowledgement and blank customer Name, Designation, Date, and Signature fields.

## Auth and integrations
There is no authentication. PDF export is implemented through the browser print-to-PDF flow; no third-party integration is configured.
