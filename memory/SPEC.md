# QuoteForge living spec

NASAKTION Quote Generator is a browser-based oil and gas commercial quotation workspace. It lets a user build sales, rental, and service quotes with configurable UoM, multiple currencies, optional tax, payment terms, delivery lead time, editable customer placeholders, and notes. Rental and service lines use daily or lump-sum charge bases; sales lines use their own per-unit or line-total pricing option. Every saved quote is persisted in MongoDB and appears in release history.

The app is branded **NASAKTION Quote Generator**, generated by Zekha Anendetha. A singleton Workspace company profile stores the editable legal company name, address, email, phone, and logo. New quotations inherit that profile as a snapshot; the Quote tab contains only quote-specific identity and commercial fields.

## Data model
- `Quote`: string id, builder mode (`margin_calculator` or `quote_only`), quote-only overall cost, editable quote number, selectable release date, execution status, supplier snapshot/logo, preparer identity/contact, customer details/reference/delivery point, quotation subject, currency/USD rate, tax settings, terms, notes, quote-level commission, line items, subtotal, cost, tax, grand total, gross profit, and margin percentage. Quote Only computes margin from item-price subtotal before commission and tax; commission remains client-facing but is excluded from Quote Only profit/margin. Switching modes preserves all hidden per-line data.
- `QuoteLineItem`: description plus printable detailed specification, category (`sales`, `rental`, `service`), charge type (`daily`, `lump_sum`) for rental/service lines, sales pricing (`unit`, `line_total`) for sales lines, UoM, quantity, duration days, price method (`sell_rate`, `margin`), direct sell rate or target gross margin percentage, cost rate, cost add-on type, effective cost add-on percentage, calculated cost add-on, commission allocation, line total and line cost. Cost add-on options are None (0%), Local Tax (11%), Import Tax incl. landing cost (14%), and Custom Tax with a user-entered percentage. Margin pricing derives sell value from fully loaded cost using `loaded cost / (1 - margin %)`.

## Key flows
1. Dashboard (`/`) is titled from the saved Workspace company name as `<Company Name> Quote Generator`, credits `Generated by Zekha Anendetha - Version 1`, and places New quote directly below `1st Release`. It shows persistent quote history and clickable USD-normalized revenue, cost, profit, and weighted margin metrics. Non-USD quotes require a saved manual `1 currency = X USD` rate; missing rates are excluded and flagged. Metric dialogs list every quotation. History includes preparer, original/USD values, editable execution status, open, and permanent delete actions.
2. New quote (`/quotes/new`) inherits the persistent Workspace company profile and captures only quote-specific parties, line items, release identity, settings, and notes. Totals and P&L update live.
3. Save creates a persistent quote and opens its saved URL (`/quotes/:id`). Saved quotes can be edited and saved again.
4. Print / PDF uses the browser print dialog and produces a customer release pack composed of fixed 210 × 297 mm A4 pages. Page 1 is a dedicated Introduction & Correspondence page with supplier/customer identity, the proposal introduction, correspondence instruction, required preparer Name/Designation/E-Mail/Phone table, customer-appreciation wording, and Best Regards signature box. Pricing begins on page 2 under the dedicated `Commercial Section` title; one or more pre-paginated commercial schedule pages end with TOTAL, terms summary, and notes but no seller signature. Quotation Terms and Conditions and Customer Acceptance follow. Every visible page has its own full header and `CONFIDENTIAL · Page X of Y` footer, and long company addresses wrap inside the supplier column. The top-bar Download PDF action captures those exact rendered A4 DOM pages at high resolution with html2canvas-pro and writes them to jsPDF, preserving loaded fonts, logo object-fit, wrapping, colors, and spacing. Its filename is `<quotation-number>-<sanitized-subject>.pdf`.
5. Download PDF is always clickable once a saved quote has hydrated. If release requirements are incomplete, it opens a checklist dialog headed `Please complete the required data prior to release` and names every missing field. Required release data includes every Parties field (customer contact/company/email/location/reference, delivery point, and delivery lead time), Quotation Subject, all Release Controls except Quote Commission (title, currency, non-USD conversion rate, enabled tax rate, payment terms, and validity), saved quote/date, Workspace company, preparer identity/contact, at least one commercial line, and each line's UoM. Line-item Description is optional and never blocks save or download. Download PDF is the only release action; the former Print / PDF button is removed.
6. Commercial line entry places the Add item button below the final item. On the final line, pressing Enter in an input automatically creates and focuses a new line only after Description, positive Quantity, positive Cost, and either positive Sell Rate or Target Margin are complete. Cost Added Value is a selector: No cost add-on (0%), Local Tax (11%), Import Tax (14%) incl. landing cost, or Customize Tax with a manual percentage input.
7. Quote Builder begins with a persistent mode selector. `Quote with Margin Calculator` retains per-line Sell Rate/Margin, Cost, and Cost Added Value. `Quote Only` keeps all scope, category, UoM, sales-pricing/charge-basis, quantity, and duration fields but replaces internal pricing controls with one Sell Price input per line and one quote-level Overall Cost below the item list. Quote Only profit is item-price subtotal minus Overall Cost, and margin divides that profit by item-price subtotal; quote commission and customer tax are excluded from this internal margin calculation. Switching modes never clears hidden per-line pricing data.
8. Each quote stores its own ordered Terms & Conditions clauses. Six editable defaults support live `{{currency}}`, `{{payment_terms}}`, `{{lead_time}}`, and `{{subject}}` tokens; clauses can be added, removed, and reordered without changing other quotations. The A4 preview can be shown or hidden while editing, and Download PDF temporarily renders hidden pages before capture.
9. Quote Builder menus are independently collapsible: builder type, Customer Detail, Subject, Commercial Items, Internal Review, T&C Editor, Release Controls, and Document Identity. Expand All and Collapse All controls provide a compact one-click view without clearing any entered data.

## Auth and integrations
There is no authentication. PDF export is implemented through the browser print-to-PDF flow; no third-party integration is configured.
